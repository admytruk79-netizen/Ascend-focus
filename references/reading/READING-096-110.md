## 100–102: four methods, not four exercise stages

## 103–107: fear example and practical descriptions

Direct will: tell oneself one does not want fear and command it to disappear.
Hypnotist example: comfort, relaxation, calm, attention diversion and repeated
suggestions; personal treatment assertions are anecdotes, not verified outcomes
or an app therapy service. Autosuggestion: serious repeated confidence/fearlessness
assertions; “double” assurances when fear returns is not a numeric prescription.
Dog/club metaphor is mental illustration, not actual animal harm guidance.

Absorption example: passivity, courage imagery and role-like enactment, with
concentration recommended. On106–107 the source says learn concentration before
fighting fear and then says begin immediately. Preserve the tension, rather than
invent an objective prerequisite or automatic training gate. Other desired
qualities use changed words. Historical claims that fear is always useless,
forced suppression and blaming insufficient desire are not guided feedback.

## 107–109: existing one six-stage protocol reconciled

1. Quiet secluded location, best available if ideal silence unavailable.
2. Comfortable resting position, relaxation; original slow/deep breaths with
   unspecified retention before exhalation until calm (historical only).
3. Inward attention; concentration exercises prepare for it.
4. Calm persistent attention to a quality word and meaning; fearlessness example.
5. Picture oneself possessing/acting with that quality, including interactions;
   end with I am and alternate imagery with that affirmation.
6. Repeat often, including before sleep/during insomnia; permit drowsiness.
