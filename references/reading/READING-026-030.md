## Four categories — original clauses and English rendering

The label I–IV describes **ways thought supposedly helps**, not four exercises.
There are no setup/action/rest/repetition stages, timers or advancement thresholds.

### I — direct suggestion, PDF28 / printed16

> Примѣненіемъ силы вашей положительной мысли въ смыслѣ прямого, личнаго воздѣйствія на людей при посредствѣ закона внушенія.

English: “By applying the power of your positive thought in the sense of direct,
personal influence on people through the law of suggestion.” The following
sentences describe interesting others in intentions, obtaining help and patronage,
and influencing them. They do not specify how to perform an exercise. Consent
safeguards are not supplied here; a contemporary respectful communication practice
would be a separately sourced and labelled adaptation.

### II — supposed thought waves, PDF29 / printed17

> Силою непосредственныхъ мыслительныхъ волнъ, приведенныхъ въ колебаніе вашимъ умомъ

English: “Through the power of direct thought waves set into vibration by your
mind.” The continuation asserts effects on other minds, including resistance,
and suggests knowledge of the law would help resist influences from others.
These are historical remote-influence claims, not scientific findings or a modern
account of brain signals. No covert-influence practice is activated.

### III — supposed attraction, PDF29 / printed17

> Путемъ притягательнаго свойства мысли, воздѣйствующей на основаніи теоріи: «сходное притягиваетъ сходное».

English: “Through the attractive property of thought, acting on the theory that
‘like attracts like.’” Maintaining certain thoughts is described as attracting
similar thoughts and beliefs from the surroundings. The passage provides a
claimed explanation, not a duration, repeated sequence, result measure or evidence
that thoughts attract external events.

### IV — character change, PDF30 / printed18

> Посредствомъ силы мысли въ отношеніи измѣненія вашего характера и темперамента съ цѣлью самоусовершенствованія, достигнуть для себя успѣха.

English: “Through the power of thought in changing your character and temperament,
with the aim of self-improvement and achieving success for yourself.” The promise
of change is the author's historical position; this paragraph supplies no discrete
exercise or guaranteed present-day benefit.

Terminology: «духовнаго господства» in the surrounding discussion can be rendered
“mental dominance” or more literally “spiritual dominance.” This is the book's
historical terminology, not a diagnosis or an app permission to dominate others.
The four categories may index later lessons, but must not be substituted for those
later lessons' actual protocols.

## General reader instruction — PDF30 / printed18

Russian:
> Своими уроками я постараюсь поставить васъ на вѣрный путь, но, указывая вамъ дорогу, предлагаю работать уже вамъ самимъ.

English: “Through my lessons I shall try to put you on the right path, but while
pointing out the road, I ask you to do the work yourselves.” The next sentence
repeats the general need to work for one's own benefit in this or another field.
This is a general reader instruction about personal effort, **not a new exercise**.
No duration, repetition count, progression rule or specific task is attached.

## Russian editorial footnote — PDF29–30 / printed17–18

The note starts at the star after category III on PDF29 and continues below the
main-text separator on PDF30. It discusses translating “Thoughts are Things” as
«мысли—это вещественныя реальности» rather than a literal “thoughts are things.”
It then asserts that thought processes can be photographed as patterns and argues
that thoughts are material realities comparable to sound/light waves. These
assertions are **historical editorial claims, not established findings**.

Scan-checked attribution sentence on PDF30:
> Спѣшимъ при этомъ добавить, что это примѣчаніе, а равно и нѣкоторыя другія сноски принадлежатъ нашей редакціи и въ другихъ изданіяхъ не встрѣчаются, нѣтъ ихъ также и въ подлинникѣ, что и просимъ имѣть въ виду.

Signature: «Редакція.»

English: “We hasten to add that this note, as well as some other footnotes, belongs
to our editorial staff and does not occur in other editions; nor is it in the
original, which we ask readers to bear in mind.” This attribution is stated by
the Russian edition itself. We have not compared other editions independently.
The identities of individual editorial contributors are not provided by this
signature and are not inferred.

Do not attribute the note's photographic-thought claim or its added explanation
to Atkinson's English original. Do not convert it into imagery, camera tracking or
research-trial requirements. Handwritten underlining and library stamps are not
part of the printed protocol.
