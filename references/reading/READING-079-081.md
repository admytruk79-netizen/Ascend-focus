## Relaxation and image (79)

Quiet location, comfortable sitting or lying, muscle relaxation and settled
thoughts precede calm but intent attention to the person. The source discourages
frowning/clenched fists, optionally closes eyes, and allows vague outlines when
the person has never been seen. Increasing imagery vividness is the author's
claimed indication of rapport, not experimentally verified communication.

## Wish imagery and tube (79–81)

Imagine the person carrying out the practitioner's wishes. Optional tube imagery
on80 puts practitioner and intended person at opposite ends of a long tube about
one foot in diameter. On81, a faint shadowy ring clears into an open tube end,
lengthens and becomes an imagined view through the full tube. The author
acknowledges imagery difficulty, qualitative practice and tube optionality.
No fixed duration, number of sessions, automatic mastery gate or chance baseline.

Scan-checked81 excerpt, wrapping normalized:
“Вы можете имѣть хорошіе результаты и безъ примѣненія трубы, однако лучшіе результаты получатъ тѣ, кто окажется способнымъ воспользоваться ею.”
English paraphrase: You can have good results without using the tube, although
the author says those able to use it obtain better results. The result claim
is attributed historically, not endorsed.

## Receptivity and boundaries (80–81)

respond better while positive mental states resist. These are not measurements

mentally saying “я есмь” when feeling outside influence, claiming protective
