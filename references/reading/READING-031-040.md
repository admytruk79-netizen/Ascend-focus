English: “Fourth lesson. Direct mental influence.” «Психическое» here concerns
mental/psychological influence; the heading does not establish paranormal efficacy.

## Study order — PDF31 / printed19

Russian:
> Мой же совѣтъ вамъ былъ бы по прочтеніи всѣхъ этихъ лекцій снова вернуться къ настоящей и тщательно пройти ее во второй разъ.

English: “My advice would be, after reading all these lessons, to return to the
present one and carefully go through it a second time.” This supplies an actual
prerequisite/order: finish the lessons, then reread Lesson IV. It does not specify
an interval, timer, daily repetition or exercise proficiency threshold.

## Further reading — PDF33 / printed21

Russian:

## Two-partner illustration — PDF34–39 / printed22–27

The original asks the reader to imagine two doubles participating as partners in a
business (PDF34), gives their contrasting responsibilities and responses (35–38),
and finally asks the reader to remember that picture (39). The image is explanatory;
it does not supply a timed sequence, repetitions or mastery stages.

Scan-checked closing passage, PDF39:
> Такъ, запомните же эту вышеочерченную воображаемую картину двухъ функцій или проявленій ума въ лицѣ какъ бы двухъ участниковъ-братьевъ съ активнымъ и пассивнымъ характеромъ.

English: “Thus, remember the imagined picture outlined above of the two functions
or manifestations of mind, represented as two brother-partners with active and
passive characters.” The author says this may assist suggestion and self-protection.
That is his intended use, not an empirically established outcome. This is not
silently reclassified as an attention exercise, contemporary dual-process theory,
a trauma model, a diagnosis or proof of a distinct hidden personality.

## Historical influence prescriptions — documentary only

PDF37 / printed25, scan-checked clause:
> отсюда слѣдуетъ и найти эту слабую точку его и повести прямо на нее атаку.

English: “From this it follows that one should find his weak point and direct the
attack straight at it.” The surrounding narrative concerns lowering vigilance and
susceptibility through surprise, flattery, fatigue and distraction. These are
historical manipulative instructions. No app-directed task for exploiting another
person is created.

PDF38 / printed26, scan-checked sentence:
> Никогда слово «нѣтъ» не принимайте за отвѣтъ.

English: “Never accept the word ‘no’ as an answer.” The following comparison extends
this to business and courtship, mentioning one refusal, two and even a dozen. Those
numbers describe refusals in the author's rhetoric; they are **not a repetition
schedule**. The source is preserved without replacing its meaning with a claim that
Atkinson instructed modern consent. It is excluded from guided publication.

A contemporary consent-based communication alternative would respect refusal and
end unwanted interaction. That is a modern safeguard, not an original instruction
and not an implemented module in this reading batch.

## Repetition claim — PDF38–39 / printed26–27

PDF38, scan-checked sentence:
> Внушенія пріобрѣтаютъ себѣ силу путемъ повторенія.

English: “Suggestions acquire force through repetition.” The author compares
possible delayed effects with seeds and describes returning for another meeting.
Repeated proposals are discussed qualitatively; no fixed count, interval or timer
is supplied. This is not permission to repeat unwanted requests, and the claimed
mechanism is not established by the passage.

## Confidence and beginning gently — PDF39–40 / printed27–28

The author recommends confidence in oneself and one's ability, then illustrates it
with a child learning to swim. He claims ability is already present and confidence
allows it to manifest. That analogy is not an actual swimming instruction or a
scientifically sound account of learning every skill.

PDF40, scan-checked sentence:
> Начните прежде съ легкихъ упражненій, но увѣренность должна быть съ самаго начала.

English: “Begin first with easy exercises, but confidence should be there from the
very beginning.” Which exercises, how long, and when to advance are not specified
here. It points toward other content; no missing exercise is invented. Do not turn
lack of confidence, fatigue or difficulty into failure/blame in modern guidance.
