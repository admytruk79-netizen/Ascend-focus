## I — black point, PDF52–53 / printed40–41

Scan-checked setup phrase, PDF52:
> листъ бѣлой писчей бумаги величиною въ 6 квадратныхъ дюймовъ

English: “a sheet of white writing paper measuring six square inches.” Preserve
this Russian wording; do not silently substitute a six-by-six-inch sheet or another
edition's wording. Draw a circle roughly the size of a half-rouble coin, ink it to
contrast with the white background and fasten it to the wall at seated eye level.
Place the chair in the middle of the room facing the paper.

Faithful normalized procedure:
1. Look firmly at the black spot without blinking for one minute.
2. When the eyes have rested, repeat, described as about five times. No fixed rest
   duration is given; the approximation must not become a forced exact total.
3. Leave the chair in place, move the paper roughly three feet right of its original
   position, face the wall ahead and look right at the mark without turning the
   head, for one minute. The source says «раза четыре»: about four times for this position.
4. Move the paper three feet left of the original position, look for one minute
   and repeat five times.
5. Continue for three days, then use two minutes; after another three days use
   three. Continue adding one minute every three days. Author's advised limit is
   fifteen minutes, despite mentioning people who supposedly reached twenty/thirty.

No-blinking instruction and progression are historical, not an automatic app plan.
The black-dot module now also retains the rightward approximation (about four) and preserves this protocol and its distinct modern
optional timer/natural blinking. The source's influence, apparent-eye-size and
hypnotism claims on53 are not verified benefits or eyesight improvement claims.

## II — mirror, PDF53–54 / printed41–42

PDF53, scan-checked sentence:
> Станьте передъ зеркаломъ и пристально смотрите въ отраженіе своихъ глазъ такъ, какъ было указано въ первомъ упражненіи.

English: “Stand before a mirror and gaze intently at the reflection of your own
eyes, as instructed in the first exercise.” It refers back to I, directs gradual
time increase and systematic practice. Atkinson says he prefers combining both
exercises rather than choosing only the mirror. This is a cross-reference, not a
separately specified mirror timer, repetition total or independent progression.

## III — stationary mark/head rotation, PDF54 / printed42

Stand facing a wall three feet away, attach white paper at eye level and keep eyes
on its mark while rotating the head, varying directions. Author claims development
of eye nerves/muscles; the claim is not a demonstrated outcome.

Scan-checked closing clause:
> самое же упражненіе должно производить вначалѣ очень умѣренно, не утомляя глазъ.

English: “The exercise itself should initially be performed very moderately,
without tiring the eyes.” No numerical duration/count/progression is specified.
Small optional turns, supported sitting, natural blinking and static alternatives
in the app are explicitly modern changes to the head-rotation protocol.

## IV — gaze between wall points, PDF54 / printed42

Stand with the back to one wall, looking toward the opposite wall. Move eyes rapidly
between its points: right, left, up, down, zigzags, circles and other patterns. Stop
immediately when eyes begin to tire. Before finishing, the author recommends resting
the gaze on one point. No duration, count or timed progression is supplied.

Scan-checked sentence:
> Упражненіе это слѣдуетъ немедленно прекратить, какъ только глаза станутъ уставать.

English: “This exercise should be stopped immediately as soon as the eyes begin
to tire.” The historical rapid movements differ from the app's slow optional
self-paced shifts; screen motion is modern, not a paper/wall instruction.

## V — friend/animal gaze experiments, PDF54–55 / printed42–43

Historical prerequisite: having acquired a firm gaze, develop confidence in it.
The author asks the reader to persuade a friend to permit testing that gaze.

Scan-checked clause, PDF54:
> для этого уговорите кого-нибудь изъ вашихъ друзей позволить испробовать на немъ вашъ взглядъ.

English: “For this, persuade one of your friends to allow you to try your gaze on
him.” Permission is present in the text. It is not a complete contemporary consent
or withdrawal protocol, age policy or guarantee of freely given ongoing agreement.

Normalized source procedure: seat the friend opposite on a chair, sit opposite,
look calmly/intently/firmly into the friend's eyes, and ask the friend to look back
as able. Author predicts fatigue and says that when the friend cries “enough,” they
will already be nearly hypnotized. The paragraph does **not** explicitly prescribe
what action to take upon that cry; do not invent a historical stop instruction or
say the author explicitly directs continuing after it.

PDF55 continues with trying the gaze on a dog, cat or another animal if it can be
made to remain still; author predicts many will flee or turn away. These are
historical animal/influence claims, not approved app experiments. No numerical
session duration, repetitions or progression rule appears for V.

A modern partner version would require voluntary participation, comfortable eye
contact or alternatives, natural blinking and immediate stopping at withdrawal;
animal/stranger tests and fatigue/hypnosis goals would be excluded. Those are
modern changes. Paired service remains unavailable, and this newly scan-checked
source record is not activated as a live paired module.

## Introductory experiments and closing advice — PDF51,55–56

PDF51 asks for experiments with people encountered and observation of effects;
it does not provide controlled targets, independent submissions, blinding or
explicit per-person consent. That passage is documentary only, not an app field
challenge. V's friend-permission wording must not be silently extended to this
separate introductory advice.

PDF55, scan-checked sentence:
> Сохраняйте тайну про себя, чтобы ваша сила проявлялась на дѣлѣ, но не на словахъ.

English: “Keep the secret to yourself, so that your power manifests in deeds rather
than words.” The surrounding advice discourages discussing the gaze exercises or
personal-magnetism studies. This is a historical secrecy prescription, not a
reason to conceal practice from participants. Private journaling is a modern data
choice, not the same mechanism as this advice.

PDF55 also asks readers to set aside practice time, avoid unnecessary haste and
develop gradually. No universal daily duration or calendar is specified in that
closing advice; exercise I's precise three-day progression is a separate passage.

PDF56, scan-checked sentence:
> Избѣгайте мигать, щуриться или неестественно расширять вѣки.

English: “Avoid blinking, squinting or unnaturally widening the eyelids.” This
chapter-wide historical advice is now retained in the app's historical context.
Guided mode continues to allow natural blinking and stop on discomfort.

PDF56, scan-checked sentence:
> Если ваши глаза отъ упражненій будутъ утомляться, то сполосните ихъ холодной водою, и тотчасъ же появится облегченіе.

English: “If your eyes become tired from the exercises, rinse them with cold water,
and relief will immediately appear.” This is the author's historical advice and
promise, not app medical guidance. His prediction of less fatigue after a few days
is also attributed, not evidence of safe forced staring or improved eyesight.
