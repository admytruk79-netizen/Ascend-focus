## Inclusion policy

Keep historical commentary, adapted practice and modern research distinct. Related books should enter an optional reading library only after title and edition verification. Extract and cite any proposed exercises individually; a cited book is not automatic authorization to attribute new protocols to Atkinson.

For a future modern evidence section, review research on attention training, task switching, listening and habit formation. Do not label the current adaptations evidence-based until suitable research and its limits have been reviewed.
