Observed 10–12 October 2026 through public primary product pages and publication
records. No competitor app was installed or independently accessibility-tested.
Pricing, proprietary algorithms, actual onboarding steps, privacy implementation,
community capabilities and WCAG scores remain UNKNOWN unless stated below. Product
claims are observations about marketing/documentation, not independently proven
outcomes. No competitor screenshots are fabricated.

## Research-to-exercise edges

| Publication / source type | Mechanism edge | Supported interpretation / limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lutz et al. 2008, Attention regulation and monitoring in meditation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18329323/), theoretical review | Focused attention → selecting an object, noticing distraction, returning attention; open monitoring → nonreactive awareness | Supports a mechanism vocabulary for modern guidance. Not a trial of Atkinson exercises, a clinical recommendation or proof of cross-task transfer. Reviewed publication record and accessible summary; full systematic extraction pending |
| [Simons et al. 2016, Do Brain-Training Programs Work?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27697851/), evidence review | Practiced-task improvement versus transfer to untrained/everyday functioning | Keep task improvement distinct from broad benefit claims. This review does not measure ASCEND, prove all training ineffective, or establish gaze/telepathy claims |
| Atkinson primary scan, Lessons VII/X/XII/XIV | Historical gaze, voluntary movement, object exploration, person/self imagery and affirmation | Documentary evidence for instructions only. Modern sound/touch alternatives, relaxed refocusing and optional timers are separate adaptations |

Reader experiences remain subjective journal observations. No subjective rapport
or imagined reply becomes a factual communication result. No neurological mechanism
is diagnosed from practice time or comfort. A title resemblance creates no evidence
edge; match stimulus, task, response, sensory load and outcome separately.

Planned review: full texts and study-specific population/sample/control/outcome
extraction; motor control, memory/mental imagery and rotation protocols; expectancy,
blinding and preregistration; historical traditions with documentary provenance;
hands-on competitor screenshots/settings/keyboard audits. Each graph edge needs a
source, reviewer, confidence and relation (mechanism-adjacent / superficial /
competing-hypothesis / no-evidence). Numerical Nielsen/WCAG competitor scores are
withheld until actual interfaces and test journeys have been assessed.
