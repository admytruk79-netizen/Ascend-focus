SCOPE AND TRUTHFUL STATUS
This is the intended complete product architecture, including functions not yet developed. At present, six coded exercise definitions exist, alongside 23 identified historical practice components in a partial source ledger. This document supplies implementation designs for those verified parts and a systematic method for extracting every remaining instruction. The full 138-page source has not yet been independently transcribed and checked line by line; no missing historical text or page number shall be fabricated. Historical mental-transmission and personal-magnetism claims must be identified as Atkinson's beliefs, not verified scientific facts.


1. PRODUCT PURPOSE
Create a serious daily-practice school of attention and thought discipline, rather than a collection of minigames. Users may practice alone without an account, review original Russian-edition source provenance, select safe adapted guidance, record experiences, pursue progressive exercises, and optionally join moderated circles or consent-based research-style paired experiments. No clinical diagnosis, ADHD treatment promise, eyesight improvement promise or proof of paranormal powers.


2. AUDIENCES AND JOBS TO BE DONE
A. Independent practitioner: enter a short practice without distraction, complete exercise, reflect privately.
B. Historical student: read original exercise faithfully, compare source against modern interpretation, preserve lesson and PDF page.
C. Neurodivergent user: choose brief sessions, movement alternatives, static targets, audio/text flexibility and no punitive streak.
D. Community member: join a circle, attend practice, share only selected journal material.
E. Facilitator: arrange permitted sessions, moderate responsibly and provide structured practice.
F. Research participant: voluntarily submit blinded experiment results without revealing target before commitment.
G. Moderator/admin: enforce consent, process reports, handle deletion/export and audit research-integrity events.

5. HISTORICAL SOURCE EXERCISE REGISTER (confirmed from selected PDF passages; paraphrase)
LESSON VII — GAZE (PDF pp 51–56)
VII-01 Black dot: prepare white paper and contrasting dark circle at eye height, focus on point, shift its placement horizontally, historically increase duration. Adapt: blink naturally, brief sessions, stop on discomfort; optional onscreen stationary point.
VII-02 Mirror observation: look at one's own reflection deliberately. Adapt: normal blink, comfortable distance and optional pause.
VII-03 Head movement with stationary marker: keep visual attention to mark while turning head. Adapt: slow limited movement, avoid dizziness and offer static alternative.
VII-04 Eye movements: deliberately scan wall points horizontally, vertically and across patterns. Adapt: optional controlled target movement with adjustable pace and reduced-motion.
VII-05 Partner gaze: historical mutual gaze experiment and animal references. Adapt: opt-in consenting human only, never coerce or distress others.
LESSON IX — WILL / REPORTED INFLUENCE (PDF pp 69–74)
IX-01 Historic imagined influence on pedestrian to turn; adapted to consent-based symbolic prediction rather than unwitting strangers.
IX-02 Imagining seated person turning in a gathering; adapted private visualization or consenting test.
IX-03 Imagining attention from someone not looking; no covert influence challenge.
IX-04 Word transmission when a speaker searches for a word; adapted paired sealed word-guessing trial.
IX-05 Imagining another person's walking direction; app uses virtual movement or consent-based predictions, not interventions in real walking.
IX-06 Imagining passerby noticing observer from a window; adapted imagery exercise, not stranger targeting.
LESSON X — TELEPATHIC WILL (PDF pp 75–85)
X-01 Quiet posture and body relaxation: sit or lie comfortably; relax muscles; settle thought.
X-02 Image of distant person: form a clear mental picture. No inference that visualization proves reception.
X-03 Imagined communication: rehearse an intended interaction with attention to the imagined person.
X-04 Long hollow tube/cylinder: visualize an imagined corridor as attentional anchor; stages: faint outline, clearer tube, imagined distant person, return attention.
X-05 Personal agency: historical affirmation 'I am' as symbolic mental boundary; avoid affirming claims of unwanted external control.
LESSON XIV — CONCENTRATION (PDF pp 120–127)
XIV-A1 Relaxed seated stillness; historical progression five to ten to fifteen minutes. Adapt voluntary movement and shorter options.
XIV-A2 Hold arm at shoulder height and observe fingers; original progresses from one minute to longer periods. Adapt shorter, easier posture and support.
XIV-A3 Brim-full glass of water in extended hand; adapt unbreakable cup partly filled, rest when needed.
XIV-A4 Observe/reduce habitual restless movements as historical prescription; adapt body awareness without shaming fidgeting.
XIV-B1 Movement concentration sequence: text on PDF page 123–124 needs manual final source verification; do not assert details before checking the scan.
XIV-B2 Rotate thumbs slowly with fingers interlaced, concentrating on movement (PDF 124).
XIV-B3 Move extended index finger slowly side to side while watching it (PDF 124).
XIV-C Deliberately study an ordinary pencil for five minutes, considering properties, purpose and origin (PDF 125–126).
OTHER LESSONS I–VI, VIII, XI–XIII, XV
Read and classify complete passages as explicit exercises, preparatory instructions, daily practice prescriptions, explanatory theory or historical claims. No invented lesson exercises. Lesson XV (PDF 128 onward) is primarily concluding instruction in inspected pages. Completion criterion is audit of ALL 138 scan pages; source page images reviewed where OCR uncertain. The 23 entries above are an initial partial source register.

7. EXERCISE PLAYER FINITE STATE MACHINE
States: IDLE → READY → RUNNING ↔ PAUSED → COMPLETED → REFLECTION → SAVED.
RUNNING → STOPPED (user stops). Any active state → INTERRUPTED (background/app crash), with restore choice.
Transition guards:
startAllowed = contentValidated AND userAcceptedSafety AND supportedDeviceFeatures AND NOT blockedByFatalError.
pairedTrialStartAllowed = authenticated AND bothConsented AND roomOpen AND targetCommitted AND NOT expired.
finishAllowed = exerciseStarted AND (explicitFinish OR durationElapsed). Always allow early, nonpunitive stop.
saveAllowed = storageAvailable OR ephemeralFallbackAccepted.
On app background, record monotonic elapsed and suspend cue playback. On resume recompute remaining time rather than using number of setInterval ticks. Snapshot every significant transition; avoid multiple concurrent timers.
Reset clears timer state, not stored journal entries. No completion counted twice; session IDs ensure idempotent saves.




8. DECISION GATES AND POLICY ENGINE (NORMATIVE)
Every gate produces ALLOW | DENY | REQUIRE_CONSENT | FALLBACK; a denied gate emits a stable reason code. Fail closed for sharing, communications and blinded research; fail soft to local practice if the network is unavailable.
G1 SOLO_START = exercise.validated AND exercise.hasGuidedSteps AND user.safetyAcknowledged AND device.storageUsableOrEphemeral. No login needed.
G2 CHOOSE_VISUAL = user.motionEnabled AND NOT system.prefersReducedMotion AND NOT user.quietMode AND exercise.permitsMotion. Else render static accessible alternative. Never automatically turn motion back on.
G3 AUTO_PROGRESS = session.finished AND user.optedIntoProgression AND selfReportedComfort>=threshold AND prerequisitesSatisfied. OR user manually chooses a suitable accessible variant. Never infer clinical improvement.
G4 COMMUNITY_READ = authenticated AND accountActive AND (circle.publicRead OR activeMembership). Anonymous visitors receive a sign-in action, not fabricated content.
G5 POST = authenticated AND member AND NOT blocked AND contentValid AND rateLimitPass AND moderationCheckPass. Denials explain action without leaking moderation rules.
G6 PAIRED_CREATE = authenticated AND ageEligible AND consentVersionAccepted AND participantNotBlocked AND trialTemplateValidated.
G7 PAIRED_LOCK = senderCommitted AND receiverCommitted AND bothConsented AND trialOpen AND serverVerifiedIdentities. Receiver must not receive target material before seal.
G8 REVEAL = submissionsLocked AND targetCommitmentVerified AND revealTimeReached. Reveal operation transactionally idempotent.
G9 PUBLIC_SHARE = authenticated AND explicitPerItemOptIn AND contentOwner AND moderationPass; private journal never automatically becomes a post.
G10 DELETE_EXPORT = accountOwnerAuthenticated OR verifiedLocalDeviceOwner; portable export of all owner data; deletion includes dependent content by specified retention policy.
G11 SUBSCRIPTION = entitlementVerified OR featureFree; refusal to purchase must never block access to free solo exercises, consent withdrawal or private export.
G12 SAFE_GAZE = userCanStopAtAnyTime AND noForcedBlinkSuppression AND staticAlternativeAvailable AND screenNotExcessivelyBright.
Formal example: CAN_START_PAIRED = AUTH ∧ AGE ∧ CONSENT ∧ PARTNER ∧ SERVICE_READY ∧ ¬BLOCKED ∧ ¬EXPIRED.
On backend outage, CAN_START_PAIRED=false; CAN_START_SOLO may remain true.
Decision table: network offline → solo yes, remote community no; reduced motion enabled → stationary target; unverified historical passage → visible draft to editors only; journal share not explicitly approved → deny; abandoned receiver trial → time out without biased reporting; conflicting submissions → preserve originals, require moderation/audit.


9. TIMING, FEEDBACK, ADAPTATION AND MATCHING ALGORITHMS
Time source: persist startedAtWallClock, elapsedMonotonicMs while foreground, pausedAccumulatedMs, lastTransition; reconcile app background conservatively. Never count hidden/background time as active attention training without explicit user approval.
Practice score is descriptive, not intelligence: completionRatio = min(activeSeconds/targetSeconds,1); practiceConsistency = sessionsInSelectedWindow / userChosenSessions; effort and discomfort stay self-reported and must not become a punitive leaderboard. Separate exercise accuracy from time and personal reflection. Raw performance histories remain private.
Adaptive selector input = consented user preference, available time, history, accessibility settings, goals, preconditions, discomfort and task performance. Hard filter safety first; then rank eligible candidates: utility = .3 goalMatch + .2 historyFit + .2 preferenceMatch + .15 accessibilityFit + .15 novelty minus fatiguePenalty. Weights are provisional design constants to test, not scientifically validated coefficients. Expose 'Why this practice?' explaining recommendation, with override and ability to opt out of adaptive ranking.
Cross-method matching engine: compute tags for objective, task stimulus, response, sensory load, duration, attentional control, training context and measurement; candidateSimilarity = weighted overlap but never automatically assert scientific equivalence. Keep human-reviewed relation types: mechanism-adjacent / superficially-similar / competing-hypothesis / no-evidence. Rank public sources by methodology and provenance, not search popularity. Mark each proposed modern variation as distinct from Atkinson's source.
Feedback loop: Plan -> Perform -> Pause or Stop -> Self-report -> Summarize descriptive signals -> Offer optional next practice -> Periodic review. No diagnostic labels.
Paired trial:
(1) server chooses target with cryptographic RNG and random target-set version;
(2) store SHA-256 commitment of server nonce+target+trialId before participants begin;
(3) sender sees assigned target, receiver sees no target and submits guess with timestamp;
(4) freeze receiver response; verify commitment on reveal;
(5) compute matches including failures, missing responses and chance expectation; never selectively omit misses;
(6) allow audit/export; report binomial uncertainty and trial counts. No claims of telepathic efficacy from small samples.
Invalid cases: nonce reuse, unblinded disclosure, receiver edit after lock, clock manipulation, missing target commitment, participant collusion warnings, repeated target leakage. Flag as invalid but retain an audit event.
Experience feedback schema: {sessionId, subjectiveFocus1to5?, comfort1to5?, distractionCount?, notes?, perceivedTransfer?, reviewedAt}; every field optional. Anxiety/overstimulation -> suggest ending, changing sensory load or taking break, without medical assessment.


10. TECHNICAL SYSTEM LAYERS
L0 Sources: original PDF, translation ledger, page verification state, research provenance.
L1 Curriculum compiler: typed Exercise schema + validators + revisioned exercise catalog.
L2 Domain: plan selection, progress calculation, session state machine, experiment orchestration.
L3 Local adapters: IndexedDB for sessions/journal; explicit schema migrations; export/delete; storage quota handling.
L4 Remote API: users, circles, reports, experiments, audit logs; server is authority for permissions/target secrecy.
L5 UI: React + TS, accessible component library, theme tokens, animation tokens, keyboard/screenreader support.
L6 Delivery: Vite + PWA; Capacitor Android/iOS; native plugin adapters isolated from web domain logic.
L7 Operations: CI, security scans, tests, logging, privacy and consent reviews, release gating.
Architecture rule: source book text and research evidence are data, not instructions to runtime; never make uncontrolled model-generated curriculum production-active.
UI cannot directly query privileged database service keys. Backend validates identity and every operation, not just client-side hidden controls.
Services and interfaces:

PracticeRepository.begin(session): SessionId; .appendTransition(event): storedEvent; .finish(id,summary): Receipt;
JournalRepository.save(entry): Receipt; .exportAll(): JSON; .delete(id): Receipt;

Application layers: screen -> view model -> domain event -> repository adapter -> IndexedDB/API -> result -> view; any failure returns typed codes with retry/undo where safe.

13. SECURITY AND ABUSE CASES
Threats: impersonation, unwanted mental-influence claims directed at members, harassment, grooming, private journal exposure, target leakage, selective study reporting, replay of answers, pseudonymous brigading, cross-device sync collision.
Mitigation: verified authentication, report/block, rate limits, moderator audit, content review queues, account deletion, community rules, consent logs; no public profiles for minors; legal review before opening multiuser service.
Experiment claims must display limitations and prevent targeting users without consent. Retain withdrawal option; withdrawal removes future involvement and handles past anonymized statistics according to consent policy.

15. NEUROINCLUSIVE INTERACTION STANDARD
No assumption that all ADHD, autistic or dyslexic users have one sensory profile. Provide preferences instead of labels or diagnostic onboarding. One clear primary action per screen; visible next step; no animated distractions during instructions; text + spoken guidance optional; timers adjustable and hideable; clear success confirmation; do not shame fidgeting or missed days.
Sensory accommodations: sound off default, no flashing, no bright pulsation, low contrast alternative for light sensitivity while maintaining legible text, adjustable font, predictable transitions. Motion controls default to static.
Motor accommodations: keyboard controls, full button labels, large tap targets, no long-press-only actions; exercise variants for physical discomfort.
Cognitive accommodations: concise instructions with optional more detail, progress checkpoints, resume with explanation, ability to slow instruction pacing, avoid overstimulation and double tasks.
Screen reader: landmark nav, headings in order, role/status, decorative SVG hidden, essential progress accessible as text, timer does not announce every second, errors connected to field IDs, proper focus when dialogs open/close. Accessibility tests include axe and assistive technology manual review on Android TalkBack and iOS VoiceOver.

17. FEEDBACK, PERSONALIZATION AND OUTCOME EVALUATION
Event collection local by default: session_started, stage_completed, paused, ended, resumed, reflection_saved, adaptation_changed, practice_bookmarked, source_viewed. Users control optional aggregated telemetry; sensitive journal text is never analytics payload.
Suggestion feedback asks 'helpful / not helpful / skip', plus explanation optional; algorithm re-ranks only with consent and never treats self-reported telepathy outcome as objective success.
Progress charts: exercise-specific duration, participation, optional objective recall accuracy, user-perceived transfer and comfort tracked separately; never aggregate into a deceptive single intelligence score.
Peer comparison disabled by default; no public vulnerability/mental health badges; experiments report all eligible completed trials, missing trials, preregistration, chance baseline and uncertainty. For 5 evenly weighted choices, chance 20%, assuming independent unbiased design. Repeated trials and post-hoc selection require correction/clear annotation.


18. EXERCISE MODULE CONTRACT / CONTENT PIPELINE
Source audit queue: each of 138 PDF pages assigned status not_reviewed -> OCR_inspected -> page_image_checked -> translated -> peer_reviewed -> publishable. Page image review required for unreadable OCR. Paragraph-level traceability source page and printed page.
Extractor identifies actionable steps, prerequisites, timing and repetitions, pauses/termination, subject consent, original author's claims. Compare against adjacent narrative; do not turn all philosophical statements into exercises.
Exercise review rubric: historical fidelity, exact source provenance, instruction completeness, accessible variant, physical safety, consent, suitability of interaction, measurable-but-not-overclaimed feedback, editorial approval.
Publishing gate = source Verified AND guidedStepsComplete AND safetyReviewed AND accessibilityReviewed AND contentSchemaValid. Historical-only speculative experiences can publish with explicit historical label and outcome limitations. Do not ship uncertain OCR as exact quotes.
Structured source sample:
{id:"atk-xiv-a1",edition:"ru-1910",source:{lesson:"XIV",pdfPages:[120,121],status:"verified-excerpt"},title:"Comfortable Stillness",historical:{duration:"5 minutes initially, then 10, then 15",attributed:true},guided:{durationDefaultSeconds:120,steps:["Choose a comfortable position","Notice breathing naturally","Rest or move whenever needed"]},capabilities:{visualTarget:false,audioOptional:true},evidence:{category:"historical-practice",medicalClaims:false}}
Exercise type variants: visual fixation, discrete eye-motion pattern, stillness, controlled voluntary movement, physical-object observation, mental imagery, interpersonal reflection, consensual experimental trial, general guided awareness. Only exercises with suitable affordances get visual animations.
